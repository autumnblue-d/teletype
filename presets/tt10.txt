GRIDS DRUMS

1: MAP X LEFT
2: MAP X RIGHT
3: MAP Y DOWN
4: MAP Y UP
5: LESS DENSE
6: MORE DENSE
7: RESET STEP TO 0
8: JUMP TO RANDOM SPOT

MUTABLE GRIDS DRUM MAP

METRO CLOCKS 32 STEPS:
TR 1 = BASS DRUM
TR 2 = SNARE
TR 3 = HI-HAT
TR 4 = ACCENT GATE
CV 1 = BD VELOCITY (GR.L)

X Y PICK THE MAP SPOT,
D SETS DENSITY. GR.P
FIRES, GR.A ACCENTS.

PARAM KNOB SETS TEMPO.
